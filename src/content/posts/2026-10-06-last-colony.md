## Why did I read it?
I read the first two books in John Scalzi's Old Man's War series, [_Old Man's War_](https://blog.samrhea.com/posts/2024/old-mans-war/) and [_The Ghost Brigades_](https://blog.samrhea.com/posts/2024/ghost-brigades/), back in 2024 and finally picked the series back up.

### Publisher Summary

Retired from his fighting days, John Perry is now village ombudsman for a human colony on distant Huckleberry. With his wife, former Special Forces warrior Jane Sagan, he farms several acres, adjudicates local disputes, and enjoys watching his adopted daughter grow up.

That is, until his and Jane's past reaches out to bring them back into the game--as leaders of a new human colony, to be peopled by settlers from all the major human worlds, for a deep political purpose that will put Perry and Sagan back in the thick of interstellar politics, betrayal, and war.

## Notes - No Spoilers
* Enjoyed it a lot more than the first two in this series! Had fun with the Obin and the world building.
